As a part of Conditions of a Necessity assembly, [invited artists](https://conditionsofanecessity.net/stream/klasse-serbest/) establish a collective domestic sphere in form of a pillow talk (Bettgeflüster) performance. A large blanket was made to sit together underneath and continue to read, discuss and actualize spatial ideas for our time, in which many have no access to any domestic sphere.
The performance contains sonic fragments, texts from the publication, dedicating the space as a mean for reconsidering the domestic sphere.

- Contributions: Abdulghaffar Tammaa, Carla Anacker, Daryna Demianchuk, Farzad Golghasemi, Idil Bozkurt, Rahel Jüngling, Tobias Babl
- Assembly: [Conditions of a Necessity](https://kunsthalle-baden-baden.de/en/programm/conditions-of-a-necessity-2) – Kunstahlle Baden-Baden, Germany
- September 2020
