*Pre-late Corrupted Abstraction* is an audiovisual lecture performance by Farzad Golghasemi and Abd Tammaa. It starts from the lion as a symbol of authority and territory which draws on nostalgia for a glorified dynastic past. The performance treats abstraction, as it operates in mathematics, topology, and 3D software, as the procedure through which such a symbol is produced, and asks what that procedure
leaves out. The text is written in dialogue: Farzad speaks from abstraction and the figure of the lion, while Abd responds from corruption, understood as what abstraction excludes and what returns to disturb the abstracted form.
