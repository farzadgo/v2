Comprising of a large lip, folded from blankets, an archive of fragmentary research materials, and the print publications ‘Muhatap’ (‘Interlocutor’), the exhibition forms a permeable body of works. It opens up a collaborative process of speculating, discussing, creating and sharing space on the topic of Queer
Ecologies, a field best described as an open invitation to intimacies with other things and beings. To critically reflect upon, expand and deconstruct ideas on nature, queer theory and sexuality, on transanimalities, utopic gardens, feminisms.

- Contributions: Abdulghaffar Tammaa, Bonnie Wenzke, Emilia Sting, Farzad Golghasemi, Feng Zeng, Frank Wang, Hanna Stijnen, Kaja Poestges, Kate Chen, Livia Brocke, Lucy Liang, Maike Sophie Quenzer, Miljana Nikovic, Peng Liu, Recep-Ali Özyilmaz, Sanija Klavina, Yilei Sheng
